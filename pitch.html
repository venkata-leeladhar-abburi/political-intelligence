The problem

Election surveys are expensive, slow and impossible to verify.

No proof it happened

A form on paper or a phone tells you nothing about whether the voter was real, or whether the agent filled it in at home.

Weeks to read answers

The most useful answers are spoken: “why will you vote this way?” People read and tag them by hand, one by one.

Numbers nobody trusts

Without evidence or a method note, every survey is questioned for bias, by the client and by the public.

More data is collected every year. Less of it can be trusted.

Source: challenges raised in client briefings by survey and campaign teams (2026); Lokniti-CSDS method notes on verification.pi.onestop-solutions.in

The solution

One platform that hears, verifies, understands and answers.

  1. Hear

    Field agents record short voice answers on an offline Android app, in Telugu.

  2. Verify

    Nine automatic checks prove each interview was real: location, time, voice, length.

  3. Understand

    Our speech engine writes down every word and scores the mood on each issue and leader.

  4. Answer

    Clients see live maps and ask questions in plain language. Every answer shows its evidence.

From guesswork to evidence, with the voter’s own words behind every number.

Source: product specification, One Stop Solutions.pi.onestop-solutions.in

How it works

From a doorstep to a dashboard in about 90 seconds.

  1. 01

    Plan

    An admin picks seats, booths and quotas that mirror each seat by age, gender and area.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Answers are tapped; only 3–4 open answers are recorded. Works with no network.

  3. 03

    Sync

    The interview waits encrypted on the phone and uploads to servers in India when signal returns.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Geo-fence, fake-GPS, duration and same-voice checks. Doubtful ones go to a supervisor.

  5. 05

    Understand

    Telugu speech becomes text, then a score per issue and leader, with the best quote.

  6. 06

    Answer

    Dashboards update; reports download as PDF; the AI analyst answers with margins and quotes.

Challenges

What could go wrong, and the plan for each.

Noisy village audio and dialects

Tested on real field clips before launch. Unclear clips go to a human reviewer; a paid engine is the backup.

Agents faking interviews

Nine automatic checks on every interview, same-voice detection and random call-backs.

A wrong call hurts a client

Ranges with error margins and a method note on every report, never single-number promises.

Election and data law

Spoken consent under the DPDP Act 2023, data in India, and a switch that blocks publishing during the Section 126A silence period.

Demand peaks around elections

A state votes every year, media trackers run all year, and surveys beyond elections fill the gaps.

Clients slow to change habits

A paid pilot in 2–3 seats first, so the client sees proof before the full rollout.

Source: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (MeitY); Representation of the People Act 1951, Section 126A; our risk register.pi.onestop-solutions.in
